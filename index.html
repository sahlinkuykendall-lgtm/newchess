✦ Lumen ⇄ ☾ Umbra

  • Strong aspect: ×1.5 damage. Weak: ×0.75.
  • Backstab (hit from behind): ×1.5 (Nyx ×2).
  • Combo: allies next to your target join the attack.
  • SP fills from fighting. At 100 → Ultimate.
  • No dice: damage is always exactly what the forecast says.
  • 🟢 Shrines heal · 🟠 Lava burns · 🔵 Water blocks.
  • Blue tiles: where you can move. Red dashed: where you could attack. ⌖: a target you can hit right now. Purple: where an enemy can move.
  • Movement styles differ, like chess pieces: Kai and Hana walk; Rin and Pip move like a bishop (diagonals are cheap); Aiko like a king (8 ways); Goro can rush 6 in a straight line; Sora glides 5 along any line; Nyx and Mako make knight leaps over walls and enemies.
  • Terrain: sand and lava cost +1 MOV, and each level you climb costs +1. Fliers ignore it.
  • Every move shows its Reach and shape: straight lines, diagonals, knight L-jumps, cones (1 ahead + 3 wide), beams (hit everyone in the line) and blasts. Pillars, trees and basalt stop shots along a line.
  • Enemy packs wait together and all attack once one spots you — pull them carefully.
  • Drag to pan the board, pinch to zoom, 🗺 to see the whole map.
  • 🌳 Trees and 🪨 rocks block movement. Gargoyles fly over water.
